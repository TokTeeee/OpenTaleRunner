# Continent of Aeslan — World Setting & Main Storyline

---

## 1. World Overview

### 1.1 Continent Name: Aeslan

The continent of Aeslan is shaped like a dragon's wing unfurling eastward, spanning from the frigid north to the tropical south. To the west lies the vast Endless Sea; to the east, the Misty Ocean, beneath which the legendary "Sunken Continent" is said to rest.

**At the eastern tip of the continent, upon the Obsidian Wastes, stands a colossal tower that pierces the heavens.** No one knows when it was built or by whom — it predates all known civilizations. Ancient texts call it the **"Worldspine"**, and legend holds that it is a passageway connecting to higher worlds. For millennia, however, the tower's entrance has been buried beneath the Demon Lord's Obsidian Throne, beyond the reach of all.

The current version only opens the continent of Aeslan (the first world).

### 1.2 Historical Eras

| Era | Year | Major Events |
|-----|------|-------------|
| Age of Creation | ? – 3,000 years ago | The gods shaped the world; the seven elemental spirits were born. **The Worldspine already existed** — its origin unknown |
| Golden Age | 3,000 – 1,200 years ago | Ancient magical civilization flourished; the era of floating cities and subterranean ruins. Ancient mages once attempted to decipher the Worldspine's power |
| Age of Calamity | 1,200 – 800 years ago | **First Demon War**: the Abyssal Rift opened, monsters swept across the continent. The Worldspine was tainted by demonic miasma; its entrance vanished |
| Age of Heroes | 800 – 10 years ago | The Seven Heroes sealed the Abyss, founded the Seven Kingdoms, and peace returned to the continent. The Worldspine remained buried underground |
| Age of the Dark Tide | 10 years ago – Present | **The fall of Aldric Dawnstar, the Radiant Hero**, on the eve of the Second Demon War. He chose the Obsidian Throne as his stronghold precisely **because it sits directly above the Worldspine** |

### 1.3 Current Era: Age of the Dark Tide

Ten years ago, the holy knight **Aldric Dawnstar** — hailed as the "Radiant Hero" — vanished during an expedition to eliminate the remnants of the Abyss. When he returned, he had become the new Demon Lord. No one knows what he endured within the Abyss — he is no longer the hero who saved humanity, but a Demon Lord commanding an army of monsters. He established his new Demon Lord's fortress at the **Obsidian Throne** on the continent's eastern tip, and long-dormant creatures from the depths have awakened to rally to his banner.

Since then, minor monster tides have appeared across the land, villages have been attacked, and trade routes have been severed. Yet the Demon Lord's full-scale invasion has not yet begun. The Seven Kingdoms are racing to prepare for war while posting bounties through the Adventurers' Guild, recruiting every soul willing to stand and fight.

**The game begins** at this very moment, with the player entering the world.

## 3. Main Storyline & Milestone System

### 3.1 Core Storyline

```
Prologue: The Dark Tide Emerges
  │
  ▼
Chapter 1: Seven Kingdoms Prepare ─── Free exploration, growth, and power accumulation
  │
  ▼
【Milestone M1】Monster Surge ────── Large-scale monster tides erupt; regional defense battles begin
  │
  ▼
Chapter 2: Flames of War ──────── Demon Lord's army formally invades; Demon Generals appear
  │
  ▼
【Milestone M2】Four Generals Descend ─ Four regions face direct attack by the Demon Generals; grand defense battles
  │
  ▼
Chapter 3: Signal Fires of Counterattack ─ Human allied forces assemble; counteroffensive into Demon Domain
  │
  ▼
【Milestone M3】Obsidian Assault ─ Final battle; determines the story's ending
  │
  ▼
Finale: Branching Endings
  ├── Great Victory: Demon Lord defeated, territory reclaimed. Worldspine's seal broken; entrance revealed.
  │    Path to the second world opens; the chronicle records the heroes' names
  ├── Pyrrhic Victory: Demon Lord defeated but at great cost. Worldspine partially opens amid aftershocks; entrance unstable
  ├── Narrow Defeat: Demon Lord retreats gravely wounded; seal shows cracks but not fully broken
  └── Total Defeat: Demon Lord's army conquers the continent; the world enters a Dark Age. Worldspine sealed even deeper
       → Opens a "Resistance" chapter: players continue the struggle in occupied territory
```

### 3.2 Milestone Trigger Mechanism

Milestones do not trigger on a fixed timeline, but based on **the cumulative contribution of all players on the server**.

| Milestone | Trigger Condition | What Happens |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **M0: Prologue** | At least 50 characters have completed origin setup, distributed across at least 3 regions | Minor monster sightings begin in all regions; the Adventurers' Guild issues the first server-wide announcement |
| **M1: Monster Surge** | Server-wide: 2,000 completed adventure quests OR any region reaches 500 contribution points | Monster tides erupt simultaneously in all regions (defense battles open); the Demon Lord formally declares war on humanity. Demon Generals begin appearing in the story |
| **M2: Four Generals Descend** | Server-wide: 10,000 monsters slain AND at least 3 regions reach 1,000 CP | The four Demon Generals each lead an army to attack four random regions; four simultaneous grand defense battles. Players must concentrate forces to defend |
| **M3: Obsidian Assault** | At least 2 Demon Generals defeated AND total CP reaches 50,000 | The human allied forces assemble to assault the Demon Domain. All region players can join the final campaign. The battle lasts 72 real-time hours |

**Contribution Point (CP) Acquisition**:

| Action | CP Awarded |
|--------|------------|
| Complete an adventure quest | 1–5 CP |
| Slay monsters | Based on monster level |
| Explore a dungeon and return safely | Based on exploration depth |
| Participate in a regional defense battle | Substantial CP (combat-performance-based) |
| Logistical contributions to regional defense (forge weapons, supply materials) | Minor CP |
| Chronicle entry recording a major discovery / event | Bonus discovery CP |

### 3.3 Branching Ending Conditions

| Ending | Condition | Worldspine Status | Impact on Second World |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Great Victory** | During M3 battle: total CP exceeds Demon Lord's durability within 72 hours AND server-wide participating players > 100 | **Seal broken; tower doors fully open** | The second world is a domain blessed by celestial light; players enter a higher world of "Light and Order" |
| **Pyrrhic Victory** | Exceeds 72 hours to achieve, or fewer than 100 participants but ultimately victorious | **Tower doors half-open; some areas explorable but passage unstable** | The second world is one of "Chaos and Rebirth" — order not yet fully established; players must simultaneously handle first-world reconstruction and second-world mysteries |
| **Narrow Defeat** | Target CP not met within 72 hours, but shortfall within 20% | **Seal shows cracks; only a few players can venture through** | The second world is "Peril and Trial" — Demon Lord survives gravely weakened; the tower's far side is a harsh proving ground only the strongest can tread |
| **Total Defeat** | CP shortfall exceeds 20% | **Worldspine sealed deeper; entrance fully closed** | Second world inaccessible directly. Players continue the "Resistance" chapter in occupied territory, seeking ways to reopen the tower. The tower's far side becomes an "Abyssal Reflection" — a corrupted world twisted by darkness |

**Core Philosophy**: Players' collective actions determine not only the fate of the first world, but also the tone of the second.
- Great Victory → Second world is **bright, ordered, a higher-tier civilization**
- Total Defeat → Second world is **dark, twisted, a world in need of redemption**
- Intermediate outcomes → Second world is **chaotic, uncharted, a world of infinite possibility**

Regardless of the ending, the second world's **terrain, races, cultures, and magical rules** can be entirely different — for the two worlds have not communicated in millennia.

## 4. The Worldspine — Hub of Multiple Worlds

### 4.1 The Nature of the Worldspine

The Worldspine is not an ordinary structure. It is an **axis connecting multiple worlds**, an existence surpassing the understanding of the current civilization.

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Height** | Immeasurable. Rises from Aeslan's surface, pierces the clouds, and vanishes beyond the visible range. |
| **Material** | Resembles obsidian but far denser; its surface is inscribed with glowing runes belonging to no known civilization. |
| **Age** | Older than the Age of Creation. It existed before the gods shaped the world. |
| **Known Entrance** | The only currently known entrance is on the continent of Aeslan, buried beneath the Obsidian Throne. |
| **Internal Structure** | According to ancient text fragments, the interior is not a staircase but a "folded space between worlds" — each level ascended is an entirely new world. |

### 4.2 Multi-World Model

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 Unknown Worlds...            │
│                     ▲                       │
│              ┌──────┴──────┐                │
│              │  Third World  │ ← Unlocked after   │
│              │   (Future)    │   defeating the     │
│              └──────┬──────┘   second world's     │
│                     ▲          Demon Lord          │
│              ┌──────┴──────┐                │
│              │ Second World │ ← Unlocked after   │
│              │    (V2)      │   defeating the     │
│              └──────┬──────┘   first world's      │
│                     ▲          Demon Lord          │
│              ┌──────┴──────┐                │
│              │  First World │ ← Current version   │
│              │   Aeslan     │                     │
│              └─────────────┘                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Core Rules**:
1. Each world is separated by **a millennium of isolation** — no communication, no trade, no migration
2. Defeat the current world's Demon Lord → the Worldspine opens the passage to the next level
3. Each world's **setting, terrain, races, civilizations, and magic/technology systems can be entirely different**
4. New players always spawn in the highest unlocked world (or can choose any unlocked world to play in)
5. Veteran players can freely choose whether to remain in the current world or ascend further

### 4.3 Why the Demon Lord Always Occupies the Tower Entrance

This is the Worldspine's deepest mystery. The Demon Lord of each world — regardless of origin, race, or era — is ultimately **instinctively drawn to the tower** and uses it as a stronghold. Ancient sages have proposed several hypotheses:

| Hypothesis | Content |
|------------|---------|
| **Seal Hypothesis** | The Demon Lord does not occupy the tower but is **sealed by it**. The tower is a prison left by the gods; the Demon Lord is bound to its base, unable to leave their world |
| **Power Source Hypothesis** | The tower emits some primordial energy that the Demon Lord must be near to sustain their power. Defeating the Demon Lord = severing their connection to the tower |
| **Guardian Hypothesis** | The Demon Lord is the tower's "test." Only by defeating the guardian — proving a world's civilization is sufficiently mature — can entry to a higher world be granted |
| **Abyssal Reflection Hypothesis** | The tower is not only a passage upward to higher worlds, but also connects downward to the Abyss. The Demon Lord exists as a "gatekeeper" preventing things from crawling out of the Abyss — Aldric may have understood precisely this when he chose to fall |

**The truth may be gradually revealed across subsequent worlds.** Each world may provide one piece of the puzzle.

### 4.4 Reserved Future World Concepts

| World | Tentative Theme | Trigger | Setting Direction (example) |
|-------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| **First World** | JRPG Fantasy | Current version | Human Seven Kingdoms, sword & magic, fallen-hero Demon Lord |
| **Second World** | Determined by ending | After M3 completion | Great Victory → "Radiant Continent" (celestial civilization, light races)<br>Total Defeat → "Inverted Abyss" (twisted world, survival horror) |
| **Third World** | TBD | Second-world Demon Lord defeated | Envisioned: steampunk wasteland / Eastern xianxia / cyber-magic hybrid |
| **Fourth World & Beyond** | TBD | Unlocked progressively | Each new world on a 1–2 real-year release cycle |

### 4.5 Cross-World Rules

- **Characters can carry equipment and skills** across worlds (but new-world materials and rules may render old gear suboptimal)
- **Character levels and attributes are preserved** (though each world may have its own "local growth system" as a new progression dimension)
- **Chronicles are independent between worlds** (each world has its own chronicle volume, so new players joining the second world need not know the entire history of the first)
- **Ghost NPCs do not cross worlds** (a first-world ghost NPC will not appear in the second world — unless a player has left footprints in both worlds)

## 5. Treasure Caverns & Dungeon Design

### 5.1 Tiering System

| Tier | Recommended Strength | Example |
|------|---------------------|---------|
| **Novice (Lv1–3)** | Starting characters viable | Town sewers, small goblin dens |
| **Apprentice (Lv4–7)** | Basic gear + 1–2 skills needed | Abandoned mines, forest bandit fortresses |
| **Intermediate (Lv8–12)** | Mid-tier gear + skill combos needed | Ancient temple ruins, dragon lair shallows |
| **Advanced (Lv13–16)** | High-tier gear + team coordination needed | Elf-King's Tomb, Abyssal Rift entrance |
| **Legendary (Lv17–20)** | Near-max-level characters | Obsidian Throne, Four General Fortresses |

### 5.2 Dungeon Types

| Type | Characteristics | Reward Tendency |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Ruin Exploration** | Ancient civilization ruins; puzzle-solving, archaeology focus | Ancient magic, lost techniques |
| **Monster Den** | Monster lairs; combat-focused | Materials, equipment drops |
| **Labyrinth Challenge** | Multi-floor mazes; pathfinding required | Treasure chests, rare items |
| **Boss Challenge** | Single powerful enemy encounter | Exclusive gear, legendary weapons |
| **Narrative Ruin** | Contains fragments of past history | Story clues, world truths |

### 5.3 Treasure Cavern Random Generation Rules

Each region periodically spawns "Treasure Caverns" — generated server-side by algorithm (non-LLM):

- Refresh rate: 1–2 spawns per region per 24 real-time hours
- Location: randomly scattered within the region's coordinate range
- Content: randomized combinations from template pools (Terrain × 3 + Monster × 4 + Treasure × 5 + Trap × 3)
- Shared access: all players can explore, but **treasure drops only for the first discoverer** (first-come, first-served)
- Caverns disappear 24 hours after being explored

## 6. Regional Defense Battle Design

### 6.1 Trigger Conditions

Defense battles come in three tiers:

| Tier | Trigger Condition | Scale |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| **Minor Monster Tide** | Random trigger (higher probability with more active players in region) | Single region, 6 real-time hours |
| **Major Monster Tide** | After Milestone M1, random trigger | Single region, 24 real-time hours |
| **Demon General Siege** | Triggered at Milestone M2; each of the four generals attacks one region | Four regions simultaneously, 48 real-time hours |
| **Final Campaign** | Triggered at Milestone M3 | Demon Domain, 72 real-time hours |

### 6.2 Collective Contribution Mechanics

```
Defense battle begins → Server creates a "battlefield instance"
      │
      ▼
Player A is in the region → Notified that a defense battle is underway
      │
      ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Player can choose participation method:         │
│  ① Direct Combat: engage the monster army        │
│  ② Fortification: reinforce walls / set traps    │
│  ③ Logistical Support: provide supplies / tend   │
│     wounded                                       │
│  ④ Special Operations: infiltrate and sabotage   │
│     enemy command                                 │
│  ⑤ Free Action: custom input any action          │
└───────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Player action → Local PM evaluation → CP calc   │
│  · Direct Combat: CP based on combat strength    │
│  · Special Ops: CP based on skills + checks      │
│  · Logistics: CP based on value of supplied      │
│    materials                                      │
└───────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
            ▼
Upload chronicle log → Server settlement

Server settlement (every 30 minutes):
   · Aggregate all players' CP in the region
   · Compare against defense threshold
     ├── Met: display "Frontline Holding" / defense advancing
     └── Not met: frontline pushed back; partial territory occupied
   · Update region state → influence subsequent story
```

### 6.3 Handling Offline Players

- At battle start, the system notifies online players via alert
- Offline players are informed of battle progress upon next login ("Chronicle Notification")
- If the battle has already ended, offline players can read the after-action report but cannot participate in the completed historical battle
- **Ghost NPC Mechanic**: If Player A was online and participated during the battle, their ghost NPC remains on the battlefield continuing to "fight" for a period, allowing Player B who logs in later to encounter them on the battlefield

### 6.4 Defense Battle Rewards

| Outcome | Reward |
|---------|--------|
| Zone Held | All participants gain regional reputation + rare loot lottery entitlement |
| Frontline Pushed Back | Participants gain "Holdfast" title with diminished rewards |
| Region Falls | Players in that region gain "Exile" background tag and a new main quest — reclaim the homeland |
| MVP Player | The highest-contributing player in the battle is recorded in the chronicle as a "Battlefield Hero" |

## 7. Core NPCs & Faction Relations

### 7.1 NPC Character Design Principles

Every NPC on the continent of Aeslan has a complete character card — not merely a dialogue tool, but a living, breathing individual. NPC design follows these principles:

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Full Character Sheet** | NPCs have 6 attributes (STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA), skill sets (with proficiency levels), backstory, personality tags, and motivations |
| **Growable** | Key NPCs' skills can grow as the story advances (e.g., Brock learning new forging recipes, Cassandra regaining holy knight skills) |
| **Social** | Player-NPC relationships are quantified by Favor (-100~100) and 5-tier Affinity (Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Close → Ally) |
| **Have Secrets** | Each NPC has several "secrets" revealed one by one when Favor thresholds or specific conditions are met, unlocking hidden storylines |
| **Recruitable** | Certain NPCs can be recruited as temporary party members (`canBeRecruited=true`), participating in combat and exploration |

### 7.2 NPC Classification & Generation Strategy

| Type | Source | Data Volume | Storage | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|---------|-------------|
| **Template NPCs** | Server-distributed templates (~20 base types) | 50–200 per region (procedurally generated) | Server distribution; client generates on demand | Merchants / blacksmiths / innkeepers / guards / healers / scholars / hunters / farmers / priests / alchemists, etc. Region-specific NPCs are randomly generated by the client based on templates + regional culture for names / appearance / personality. |
| **Client-Exclusive NPCs** | Dynamically created by the PM during gameplay | 50–200 per player | Client creates → registers with server | Fellow villagers from origin background, family members, people met during adventures. Initially client-exclusive; after registration, not shared with other players. |
| **Promoted NPCs** | Server detects an NPC being frequently interacted with by multiple players | Grows dynamically | Server flags → broadcasts to all clients | Any template or exclusive NPC may be promoted through collective player behavior. Upon promotion, gains full backstory, new skills, and regional influence. |
| **Author-Handcrafted NPCs** | Manually added by world authors | 10–50 | Server `npc_templates.json` `author_npcs` list | Optional. Authors may add characters with complete stories to the world. These NPCs have no fundamental distinction from template NPCs. |

**Core Design Principle: NPCs have no preset "key / non-key" distinction — it is the players' collective behavior that grants NPCs their importance.**

### 7.3 NPC Promotion Mechanics

| Condition | Trigger | Effect |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| 5 unrelated players interact with the NPC | Server marks as "Noticed" | The NPC begins appearing with a "locally famous" tag in other players' regions |
| Single player interactions ≥ 20 OR Favor ≥ 80 | Client marks as promotable | After server check, generates detailed backstory and unlocks exclusive quests for the NPC |
| NPC mentioned in chronicle on 3+ world days | Server generates as "Regional Notable" | Generates expanded backstory, adds skills, becomes recruitable |
| NPC actions materially affect regional state | Server registers as "Regional Fixed NPC" | Permanently appears in the region, even when the original player is offline |
| NPC exerts non-negligible influence on the main story | Server adds as permanent chronicle character | Known server-wide; becomes part of world history |

### 7.4 Favor System

Favor is the core of the entire social system, directly influencing the depth of player-NPC interaction.

| Affinity | Favor Range | Interaction Effects |
|----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Stranger | -100 ~ 4 | NPC does not proactively offer help; high prices; reveals no secrets |
| Acquaintance | 5 ~ 24 | NPC remembers the player's name and identity; normal trade; may offer basic quests |
| Friend | 25 ~ 49 | NPC more willing to share info; occasional discounts or priority quests; may reveal 1 secret |
| Close | 50 ~ 79 | NPC proactively helps; best trade terms; reveals deeper secrets; may consider partying |
| Ally | 80 ~ 100 | NPC fully trusts the player; willing to take risks alongside; provides exclusive services (e.g., bespoke forging, special technique training); all secrets unlocked |

### 7.5 Favor Change Rules

| Action | Favor Change |
|--------|-------------|
| Help NPC complete a quest | +5 ~ +20 (based on quest importance) |
| Protect NPC in combat | +10 |
| Gift rare items | +5 ~ +15 (based on item value) |
| Friendly interaction with NPC's faction | +2 ~ +5 |
| Deceive or betray the NPC | -15 ~ -30 |
| Harm someone the NPC cares about in their presence | -20 ~ -40 |
| Prolonged absence of interaction (30+ game days) | -2 per 10 days (minimum: Acquaintance floor) |
| Reveal NPC's secret (without their consent) | -25 |
| Help NPC fulfill personal motivation | +20 ~ +30 |

### 7.6 Party NPC Rules

- `canBeRecruited=true` + Favor ≥ 60 → can invite NPC as a temporary party member
- When in party, NPC is recorded in the chronicle as "Teammate"
- Party NPCs use their own attributes + skills for independent checks during combat
- Party NPC injuries in combat can affect Favor (poor protection by the player may reduce Favor)
- Party NPCs can "graduate" upon meeting specific conditions — settling in a town and providing permanent services

## 8. Chronicle Narrative Style

### 8.1 Narrative Voice

The world chronicle is written in the voice of a **court historian**, with a style referencing JRPG opening narrations or the storytelling tone of *Fire Emblem*:

> **World Day 132 · Age of the Dark Tide**
>
> The miners of the Iron Ridge Mountains will never forget this day. When they broke through the ancient rock layer, what awaited them was not ore, but the fire dragon Mordred, slumbering for a thousand years. Hearthforge City fell into a sea of flames overnight.
>
> The adventurer "Ragg Ironhammer" led seventeen miners through the burning tunnels, successfully evacuating three hundred civilians. His name was carved onto Hearthforge's Heroes' Monument —
>
> And on that very same day, far away in the Emeraldwood, the adventuress "Irene Ash" discovered a memory shard within a forgotten elven ruin that might unveil the Demon Lord's true face. Two threads of destiny quietly intertwined in that day's chronicle.

### 8.2 Chapter Naming

Each day's generated chronicle narrative should carry a title, auto-generated by the grand PM (server LLM), for example:

- "Hearthforge in Flames"
- "Whispers of the Elven Ruins"
- "The Day of Intertwined Destinies"

## 10. Event System

### 10.1 Design Philosophy

All significant events in the world are pre-defined by "event templates" (auto-generated by the server based on the storybook), which the client PM adapts into specific scenarios for players to experience during actual gameplay. Event relationships are networked — potentially involving complex prerequisite chains, causal sequences, and cross-region linkages.

The essence of events is the **skeleton of the chronicle** — the storybook provides the world framework, event templates fill in specific plots, and player actions give them flesh.

### 10.2 Event Tiers

| Tier | Name | Count (V1) | Example |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| **Major** | Major Events | 5–10 | Four Generals descend, Demon Lord declares war, Worldspine opens |
| **Late** | Late Events | 10–20 | Generals' personal story arcs, truth-shard reveals |
| **Mid** | Mid Events | 20–50 | Faction conflict escalation, cross-region linked events |
| **Early** | Early Events | 30–80 | NPC first encounters, minor monster sightings, ancient ruin discoveries |
| **Minor** | Minor Events | 50–200 | Environmental encounters PM can adapt at any time; weather shifts, NPC chance meetings |

Approximate total: **100–300 event templates** for V1, procedurally generated by the server based on region settings + storylines.

### 10.3 Event Trigger Conditions

| Condition Type | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **world_day_X** | Unlocks after reaching a specific world day |
| **player_in_region** | Player is in a specific region |
| **npc_met** | Player has met a specific NPC |
| **events_completed** | Prerequisite events completed |
| **pm_adaptive** | PM can proactively adapt, unrestricted by other conditions (Minor / Early events only) |
| **probability** | Random probability trigger (to create a "living world" feel) |
| **faction_attitude** | Player's favor with a specific faction meets the threshold |

### 10.4 Multi-Player Collaboration Mechanics

When the client PM prepares to trigger an event:
1. **First confirm with the server** whether the event template has already been triggered by someone
2. **Not yet triggered** → Server creates an event instance; client PM generates the scene and reports an "event execution plan" to the server
3. **Already triggered** → Player participates from an alternate perspective (assisting the protagonist, investigating side clues, providing logistical support); upon completion, both narratives are merged by the server

### 10.5 Event Presentation in the Chronicle

Upon completion of each event, its actual narrative is recorded as a chronicle entry. The event's title and summary appear on the dashboard: who discovered it, current progress, whether it is completed. This allows all players to see "what is happening" in the world.

## 11. Roadmap

The continent of Aeslan already possesses a grand history spanning five eras, seven distinct regions, a four-milestone main storyline from prelude to final assault, a multi-world model centered on the Worldspine Tower, a collective-contribution regional siege battle system, and a social ecosystem woven with NPC relationships and faction dynamics.

In terms of narrative depth, we aim to further expand the story details of the fourth era, allowing the alternating ages of gold and calamity to reveal more forgotten tales. New geographical frontiers will also be opened, enabling adventurers to set foot in the northern tundra and southern isles — territories yet to be fully depicted. The NPC relationship system will extend toward richer dimensions — romantic bonds, nemesis rivalries, and master-apprentice lineages will imbue the social system with more profound emotional expression.

The second world of the Worldspine Tower will be a major milestone for the world setting — a new world whose tone is determined by the players' collective choices will be fully realized. Regions will no longer be static stages; war, plague, and prosperity will truly reshape the map. Channels for community co-creation of world lore will also open, allowing players' imagination to flow into the lifeblood of Aeslan.

In the farther future, we envision multi-world parallel operation — players freely migrating between different worlds, each with its own independent chronicle and endgame events. The fate of the world will not rest on a single moment of vanquishing the Demon Lord, but will unfold in continuous evolution, writing an epic belonging to all adventurers.
